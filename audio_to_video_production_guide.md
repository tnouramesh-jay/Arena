# 🎙️ Audio-to-Video Production Guide: From Voice Recording to Viral Short

Congratulations on recording your voice version! You have completed the most important step in creating your next **"AI ரோபோ தமிழ் கற்றுத் தருது! 🤖"** episode.

This guide shows you exactly how to combine your Vocaroo voice recording (`1op2MSdN9MuX`) with your AI Robot visuals in **CapCut or VN Editor** in under 10 minutes.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Download & Prepare Your Audio
1. **Download your MP3:** Open your Vocaroo link (`https://voca.ro/1op2MSdN9MuX`) and click the **Download** button to save the `.mp3` file to your phone or computer.
2. **Audio Enhancement (Optional - Free Pro Tip):**
   * If there is any slight background room noise or echo in your recording, upload the MP3 to **Adobe Podcast Enhance** (free at `podcast.adobe.com/enhance`). It will instantly make your microphone sound like a professional studio recording!

---

## 🎬 Step 2: Combine Audio + AI Robot Visuals in CapCut / VN Editor

### 1. Set Project Ratio to 9:16 (Vertical Short)
* Open CapCut or VN Editor and create a **New Project**.
* Set the aspect ratio to **9:16 (Vertical)** for YouTube Shorts.

### 2. Add Your Visual Track (The AI Robot 🤖)
* Import your AI Robot character graphic or background video onto the main video timeline.
* Make sure the duration of the visual track matches the exact length of your voice recording (~45 to 60 seconds).

### 3. Import Your Vocaroo Voice Recording
* Tap **Audio > Sounds > From Device** (or drag and drop your `.mp3` file).
* Align the start of your audio with the start of the video.
* **Pro Tip:** If there is any silent pause before you start speaking, trim that beginning silence so your voice starts immediately within the **first 0.5 seconds**!

---

## 🎨 Step 3: Add Bold Tamil & English On-Screen Captions

Since over 70% of YouTube Shorts viewers watch on mute or in public, large on-screen text is essential to keep them watching:

1. **When you speak the Hook:**
   * Display large text at the top: **1 முதல் 5 வரை எண்ணலாமா? 🖐️** (or **6 முதல் 10 வரை 🖐️🖐️** depending on which part you recorded).
2. **When you say each number:**
   * Add pop-up text in the center of the screen timed to your voice:
     * **1 - ஒன்று (Onru)** 🍎
     * **2 - இரண்டு (Irandu)** 🍎🍎
     * **3 - மூன்று (Moonru)** 🍎🍎🍎
     * **4 - நான்கு (Naangu)** 🍎🍎🍎🍎
     * **5 - ஐந்து (Ainthu)** 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎
   * **Text Styling Tip:** Use **Yellow or White bold font** with a **Black outline/shadow** so it stands out clearly against your AI Robot background.

---

## 🚀 Step 4: Export & Upload to YouTube Shorts

### 1. Export Settings
* **Resolution:** 1080p
* **Frame Rate:** 30fps or 60fps

### 2. YouTube Upload Metadata (Copy & Paste)
* **Title:**
  ```text
  1 முதல் 5 வரை தமிழில் எண்ணலாமா? 🖐️ Learn Tamil Numbers 1 to 5 | AI ரோபோ #Shorts #LearnTamil
  ```
  *(Note: If your recording is for Numbers 6-10, change "1 முதல் 5" to "6 முதல் 10" in the title!)*
* **Description:**
  ```text
  AI ரோபோ தமிழ் கற்றுத் தருது! குட்டீஸுக்கான தமிழ் எண்கள் (Tamil Numbers for Kids).
  உங்கள் குழந்தைகளுக்கு எளிய முறையில் தமிழ் கற்றுத்தர Subscribe செய்யுங்கள்! 
  #Tamil #LearnTamil #TamilNumbers #TamilForKids #AIRobot #Shorts #KidsTamil #1to10
  ```
* **Audience:** Select **"No, it's not made for kids"** (Always select this so YouTube keeps Comments and Notifications enabled!).
* **Category:** Education.
