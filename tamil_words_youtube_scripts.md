# YouTube Video Script Series: Learn Spoken Tamil Words Easily! 🌟

This document contains **3 ready-to-record continuation scripts** for your YouTube playlist on learning Tamil words. Each script is formatted with **Visual Cues** (what to show on screen) and **Spoken Narration** (what you say into the microphone), including pauses for viewers to repeat after you.

---

## 🎬 Episode 1: Everyday Essential Tamil Greetings & Courtesy Words

**Video Title Ideas:**
- *Learn 8 Essential Tamil Words You MUST Know! | Tamil for Beginners*
- *Speak Tamil in 5 Minutes: Greetings & Courtesy Words*
- *Common Tamil Words Every Beginner Should Know (With Pronunciation)*

**YouTube Description:**
> Welcome back to our Learn Tamil series! In today's video, we are learning 8 essential Tamil words for greetings and everyday courtesy. Practice your pronunciation along with me! Don't forget to like, subscribe, and drop a comment below with your favorite Tamil word.

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] Introduction
* **Visual Cue:** Show video intro graphic with title: **"8 Essential Tamil Words for Everyday Life"**. Your smiling presenter face or avatar on camera.
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Vanakkam everyone, and welcome back to the channel! If you've been following our Tamil playlist, you already know that learning just a few key words can completely transform your conversations. Today, we're covering eight essential greetings and courtesy words in Tamil that you will use every single day. Make sure to repeat after me out loud. Let's jump right in!"

---

### [0:30 - 1:05] Word 1: Hello / Welcome — வணக்கம் (Vanakkam)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Hello / Welcome
  * **Tamil Script:** வணக்கம்
  * **Pronunciation:** *Va-nak-kam*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Our first word is the most beautiful greeting in Tamil—**Vanakkam**. It means both Hello and Welcome. Let's say it together: **Va-nak-kam**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds for viewer to repeat)*
  > "Vanakkam! You can use this with anyone, anywhere in Tamil Nadu."

---

### [1:05 - 1:45] Word 2: How are you? — எப்படி இருக்கிறீர்கள்? (Eppadi irukkeenga?)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** How are you?
  * **Tamil Script:** எப்படி இருக்கிறீர்கள்? (Casual: எப்படி இருக்கீங்க?)
  * **Pronunciation:** *Ep-pa-di i-ruk-keen-ga?*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Next up, how do you ask someone 'How are you?' In polite spoken Tamil, we say: **Eppadi irukkeenga?** Let's break that down: **Ep-pa-di** means 'how', and **i-ruk-keen-ga** means 'are you'. Let's say it together: **Eppadi irukkeenga?**"
  > *(Pause 2 seconds for viewer to repeat)*
  > "Great job!"

---

### [1:45 - 2:20] Word 3: I am fine — நான் நன்றாக இருக்கிறேன் (Nalla irukken)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** I am doing well / fine
  * **Tamil Script:** நல்லா இருக்கேன்
  * **Pronunciation:** *Nal-la i-ruk-ken*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "When someone asks you *Eppadi irukkeenga?*, how do you reply? You say: **Nalla irukken**—meaning 'I am fine' or 'I am doing well'. Try saying it: **Nal-la i-ruk-ken**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds for viewer to repeat)*
  > "Perfect! *Nalla irukken*."

---

### [2:20 - 2:55] Word 4: Thank you — நன்றி (Nandri)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Thank you
  * **Tamil Script:** நன்றி
  * **Pronunciation:** *Nan-dri* (The 'ri' is pronounced like 'dree' with a soft r)
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Our fourth word is **Nandri**, which means Thank you. It is simple, polite, and heartfelt. Say it with me: **Nan-dri**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds for viewer to repeat)*
  > "Nandri! Say it with a smile, and locals will love you for it."

---

### [2:55 - 3:30] Word 5: Please — தயவுசெய்து (Thayavu seidhu)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Please
  * **Tamil Script:** தயவுசெய்து
  * **Pronunciation:** *Tha-ya-vu sei-dhu*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "To ask for something politely, we use the word for 'Please': **Thayavu seidhu**. Let's practice: **Tha-ya-vu sei-dhu**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds for viewer to repeat)*
  > "Excellent! *Thayavu seidhu*."

---

### [3:30 - 4:05] Word 6 & 7: Yes & No — ஆமாம் (Aamaam) / இல்லை (Illai)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **YES:** ஆமாம் (*Aa-maam*)
  * **NO:** இல்லை (*Il-lai*)
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Let's learn Yes and No! 'Yes' in Tamil is **Aamaam**—or casually, just **Aama**. Try saying: **Aa-maam**."
  > *(Pause 1.5 seconds)*
  > "And 'No' is **Illai**. Let's repeat: **Il-lai**."
  > *(Pause 1.5 seconds)*
  > "Aamaam for Yes, Illai for No."

---

### [4:05 - 4:40] Word 8: See you later — பாக்கலாம் (Paakkalaam)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** See you later!
  * **Tamil Script:** பாக்கலாம்
  * **Pronunciation:** *Paak-ka-laam*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Finally, when you say goodbye to a friend, you can say **Paakkalaam**, which means 'We can see later' or 'See you soon!'. Say it with me: **Paak-ka-laam**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Paakkalaam!"

---

### [4:40 - 5:15] Outro & Call to Action

## 🎬 Episode 2: Essential Tamil Vocabulary for Food & Dining 🍛

**Video Title Ideas:**
- *Learn Spoken Tamil for Eating & Restaurants | 7 Delicious Words!*
- *Order Food in Tamil Like a Native! | Easy Tamil Vocabulary*

---

### [0:00 - 0:35] Introduction
* **Visual Cue:** Show delicious South Indian food visuals (Dosa, Idli, Sambar, Filter Coffee) with text: **"Tamil Words for Food & Dining"**.
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Vanakkam friends! Today we are continuing our Tamil playlist with one of my absolute favorite topics—FOOD! Whether you are visiting Chennai or eating at your local South Indian restaurant, knowing these 7 food and dining words in Tamil will make your experience ten times better. Let's get started!"

---

### [0:35 - 1:10] Word 1: Water — தண்ணீர் (Thanneer / Casual: Thanni)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Water
  * **Tamil Script:** தண்ணீர் (Spoken: தண்ணி)
  * **Pronunciation:** *Than-neer* / Spoken: *Than-ni*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "The first word you need at any table is Water. In formal Tamil it's **Thanneer**, but in casual spoken Tamil, everyone says **Thanni**. Let's practice the spoken version: **Than-ni**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "If you want drinking water, you can say *Thanni vaendum*—meaning 'I want water'."

---

### [1:10 - 1:50] Word 2: Food / Meal — சாப்பாடு (Saappaadu)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Food / Meal
  * **Tamil Script:** சாப்பாடு
  * **Pronunciation:** *Saap-paa-du*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Our second word is the word for a meal or cooked food: **Saappaadu**. When someone asks if you've eaten, you'll hear this word all the time! Let's say it: **Saap-paa-du**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Saappaadu. Wonderful!"

---

### [1:50 - 2:30] Word 3: Very Tasty / Delicious — ரொம்ப நல்லா இருக்கு (Romba nalla irukku)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** It's very tasty / good!
  * **Tamil Script:** ரொம்ப நல்லா இருக்கு
  * **Pronunciation:** *Rom-ba nal-la i-ruk-ku*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Want to compliment the chef? How do you say 'It is very tasty'? In spoken Tamil, we say: **Romba nalla irukku**. **Romba** means very, and **nalla irukku** means it's good. Say it with excitement: **Romba nalla irukku!**"
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Trust me, saying this after a meal will bring a huge smile to anyone's face!"

---

### [2:30 - 3:05] Word 4: I want / More — வேண்டும் / இன்னும் (Vaendum / Innum)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **I want:** வேண்டும் (*Vaen-dum*)
  * **More:** இன்னும் (*In-num*)
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "What if you want more sambar or chutney? The word for 'I want' is **Vaendum**. And the word for 'more' is **Innum**. Put them together: **Innum vaendum**—'I want more'. Repeat after me: **In-num vaen-dum**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*

---

### [3:05 - 3:45] Word 5: Enough / That's enough — போதும் (Podhum)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Enough / No more, thank you
  * **Tamil Script:** போதும்
  * **Pronunciation:** *Po-dhum*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "In South Indian hospitality, people love serving you extra food! When your plate is full and you want to say 'Enough', you say: **Podhum**. Let's say it: **Po-dhum**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "If you want to be extra polite, say: **Podhum, nandri**—'Enough, thank you!'"

---

### [3:45 - 4:25] Word 6: How much is this? — இது எவ்வளவு? (Idhu evvalavu?)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** How much is this? (For bill/price)
  * **Tamil Script:** இது எவ்வளவு?
  * **Pronunciation:** *I-dhu ev-va-la-vu?*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "When it's time to pay the bill, how do you ask 'How much is this?' You say: **Idhu evvalavu?** **Idhu** means 'this', and **evvalavu** means 'how much'. Try saying it: **I-dhu ev-va-la-vu?**"
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Idhu evvalavu. Great job!"

---

### [4:25 - 5:00] Outro & Call to Action
* **Visual Cue:** Summary screen with all 6 dining expressions.
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Let's recap today's food vocabulary:
  > Thanni — Water
  > Saappaadu — Food/Meal
  > Romba nalla irukku — Very tasty!
  > Innum vaendum — I want more
  > Podhum — Enough
  > Idhu evvalavu? — How much is this?
  > 
  > Practice these next time you eat South Indian food! Subscribe to the channel so you don't miss Episode 3, and as always—Nandri, Paakkalaam!"

## 🎬 Episode 3: Practical Travel & Direction Words in Tamil 🚖

**Video Title Ideas:**
- *Learn Spoken Tamil for Travel | 7 Must-Know Direction Words*
- *How to Ask Directions in Tamil | Simple Tamil Words for Beginners*

---

### [0:00 - 0:35] Introduction
* **Visual Cue:** Video title: **"7 Practical Tamil Words for Travel & Directions"**. Animated map or auto-rickshaw (tuk-tuk) graphic.
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Vanakkam! Welcome back to our Tamil vocabulary series. If you are traveling around Tamil Nadu, taking an auto-rickshaw, or exploring the streets of Chennai, this video is for you! Today we're learning 7 practical words to ask for directions and navigate with confidence. Let's dive in!"

---

### [0:35 - 1:15] Word 1: Where is...? — ... எங்கே? (... Engae?)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Where is...?
  * **Tamil Script:** ... எங்கே?
  * **Pronunciation:** *En-gae?*
  * **Example:** Railway station engae? (Where is the railway station?)
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "The most important question word for travel is **Engae**, which means 'Where?'. To ask where a place is, just say the name of the place followed by *engae*. For example: *Railway station engae?* or *Hotel engae?* Let's practice saying 'where': **En-gae?**"
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Engae. Super useful!"

---

### [1:15 - 1:55] Word 2 & 3: Here & There — இங்கே (Ingae) / அங்கே (Angae)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **HERE:** இங்கே (*In-gae*)
  * **THERE:** அங்கே (*An-gae*)
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Once you ask *Engae*, someone might point and say 'Here' or 'There'. 'Here' in Tamil is **Ingae**. And 'There' is **Angae**. Notice how similar they are! Let's practice both:
  > Say Here: **In-gae**"
  > *(Pause 1.5 seconds)*
  > "Now say There: **An-gae**"
  > *(Pause 1.5 seconds)*
  > "Ingae and Angae!"

---

### [1:55 - 2:35] Word 4 & 5: Left & Right — இடது (Idadhu) / வலது (Valadhu)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **LEFT:** இடது பக்கம் (*I-da-dhu pak-kam*)
  * **RIGHT:** வலது பக்கம் (*Va-la-dhu pak-kam*)
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "How about Left and Right? 'Left' is **Idadhu**, and 'Right' is **Valadhu**. The word **pakkam** means 'side'. So **Idadhu pakkam** is 'left side', and **Valadhu pakkam** is 'right side'. Let's repeat Left Side: **I-da-dhu pak-kam**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "And Right Side: **Va-la-dhu pak-kam**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*

---

### [2:35 - 3:10] Word 6: Stop here — இங்கே நிறுத்துங்க (Ingae niruthunga)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Please stop here
  * **Tamil Script:** இங்கே நிறுத்துங்க
  * **Pronunciation:** *In-gae ni-ruth-thun-ga*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "When you're in an auto-rickshaw or taxi and you reach your destination, how do you say 'Stop here'? You say: **Ingae niruthunga**. We already know **Ingae** means 'here', and **niruthunga** means 'please stop'. Let's practice: **In-gae ni-ruth-thun-ga**."
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Ingae niruthunga!"

---

### [3:10 - 3:45] Word 7: Let's go — போகலாம் (Pogalaam)
* **Visual Cue:** On-screen text:
  * **English:** Let's go / We can go
  * **Tamil Script:** போகலாம்
  * **Pronunciation:** *Po-ga-laam*
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "Finally, when you're ready to leave or start moving, you can say: **Pogalaam**—meaning 'Let's go!'. Say it with energy: **Po-ga-laam**!"
  > *(Pause 2 seconds)*
  > "Pogalaam!"

---

### [3:45 - 4:20] Outro & Call to Action
* **Visual Cue:** Recap screen showing: Engae, Ingae, Angae, Idadhu, Valadhu, Ingae niruthunga, Pogalaam.
* **Spoken Narration (Your Voice):**
  > "You did fantastic today! Let's review:
  > Engae? — Where?
  > Ingae / Angae — Here / There
  > Idadhu / Valadhu — Left / Right
  > Ingae niruthunga — Stop here
  > Pogalaam — Let's go!
  > 
  > You are now ready to navigate Tamil Nadu like a pro. Thank you so much for watching—Nandri! Don't forget to subscribe and turn on notifications for our next lesson. Paakkalaam!"

## 💡 Pro Tips for Recording Your Voiceover
1. **Warm, Conversational Tone:** Speak as if you are coaching a friend sitting next to you.
2. **Clear Pauses:** When you say *"(Pause 2 seconds for viewer to repeat)"*, leave clear silent gaps in your recording—viewers really appreciate having time to practice speaking aloud!
3. **Pronunciation Clarity:** Emphasize the syllables slowly the first time you introduce a Tamil word, then say it at natural conversational speed.
